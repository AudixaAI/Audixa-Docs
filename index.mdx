Audixa converts text into audio through an authenticated REST API. Choose a
compatible voice and model, submit a generation, and poll its status until the
audio is ready.

## How It Works

Audixa uses an **asynchronous workflow** for audio generation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit Request">
    Send non-empty text to `POST /v3/tts` with `voice_id`, `model`, and
    `audio_format`. Each generation is billed for at least 30 characters (8 tokens).
    The response includes a `generation_id` and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll Status">
    Request `GET /v3/tts?generation_id=...` until the status is `COMPLETED` or `FAILED`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Audio">
    A completed response includes `audio_url` for the generated MP3 or WAV file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Preview Before You Pay

Listen to the fixed samples at [audixa.ai/voices](https://audixa.ai/voices) to
compare voices before purchasing. These samples use pre-generated text; they do
not accept custom input and do not create a generation.

<Info>
  Audixa does not provide free generations or trial credits. Creating audio in
  the dashboard or API requires either a minimum **$10 top-up** or an active
  subscription.
</Info>
