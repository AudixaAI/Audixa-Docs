The Account page provides an overview of your profile, current plan, and critical account settings.

## Account Overview

This section displays your key account information:
- **Current Plan:** Your active subscription tier.
- **Profile Details:** Name, Email, and Account Creation date.
- **User ID:** Your unique internal ID (useful for support).

## Subscription Management
You can quickly check your plan features here. To upgrade or manage your billing
details, click **Manage Billing**.

Subscription cancellation and account deactivation are separate actions. If you
have an active subscription, use **Manage Subscription** in Billing to stop its
renewal. A scheduled cancellation remains active through the date shown in
Billing and can be revoked before that date.

## Danger Zone

<Warning>
  The dashboard action is a **soft account deactivation**. It does not cancel an
  active subscription and it does not immediately erase every database record.
</Warning>

When you confirm deactivation, Audixa:

- marks the account as deleted so it can no longer be used to sign in;
- revokes the current session and clears its session cookie; and
- attempts to remove user-uploaded files from object storage.

Account, billing, generation, custom-voice, and API-key records may remain in the
operational database for billing reconciliation, security and support work,
analytics continuity, or possible administrator-assisted recovery. Audixa does
not publish a fixed automatic-erasure period for those retained records.

Before deactivating, cancel any subscription separately in Billing and contact
support if you need help with retained data or a billing issue. Do not assume the
deactivation button has stopped a payment-provider subscription.

## Need Help?
